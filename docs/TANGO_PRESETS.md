## Recommended BPM Settings

### Traditional Tango
- **BPM Range**: 120-130 BPM
- **Tempo**: 1.0x (normal speed)
- **Characteristics**: Steady, rhythmic, dance-oriented

### Tango Nuevo
- **BPM Range**: 100-120 BPM
- **Tempo**: 0.9x - 1.0x
- **Characteristics**: Slower, more expressive

### Milonga
- **BPM Range**: 130-150 BPM
- **Tempo**: 1.0x - 1.1x
- **Characteristics**: Faster, more energetic

### Vals (Tango Waltz)
- **BPM Range**: 90-110 BPM
- **Tempo**: 0.8x - 0.9x
- **Characteristics**: Slower, three-beat rhythm

## Mixing Tips for Tango

### Crossfader Technique
1. Start with Deck 1 at full volume (crossfader left)
2. Gradually move crossfader to center over 8-16 bars
3. Complete transition to Deck 2 (crossfader right)
4. Maintain consistent BPM throughout

### Volume Balancing
- Keep master volume at 80-90% to avoid distortion
- Balance individual deck volumes for smooth transitions
- Use crossfader for primary mixing, not volume sliders

### Tempo Matching
1. Identify BPM of both tracks
2. Adjust Deck 2 tempo to match Deck 1
3. Fine-tune with tempo slider (±5-10%)
4. Listen for beat alignment

### Equalizer Adjustments
- Use equalizer to enhance complementary frequencies
- Reduce overlapping frequencies to prevent muddiness
- Gradually adjust EQ during transitions
- Return to neutral (flat) between tracks

## Troubleshooting Tango Mixing

### Issue: Tracks sound muddy when mixed
- **Solution**: Reduce overlapping frequencies using EQ
- Reduce bass on one deck while mixing

### Issue: Tempo doesn't match
- **Solution**: Use tempo slider to fine-tune
- Listen to kick drum or bass line for alignment

### Issue: Transition sounds abrupt
- **Solution**: Use crossfader more gradually
- Adjust EQ during transition for smoother blend

### Issue: Audio distortion
- **Solution**: Lower master volume
- Reduce individual deck volumes
- Check audio file quality

## Advanced Techniques

### Beat Matching
1. Identify the kick drum in both tracks
2. Adjust tempo until kicks align
3. Use headphones to monitor beat alignment
4. Fine-tune with tempo slider

### Frequency Isolation
1. Use EQ to isolate specific instruments
2. Enhance bandoneon on one deck
3. Enhance strings on other deck
4. Blend for rich, layered sound

### Dynamic Mixing
1. Start with smooth crossfade
2. Gradually adjust EQ during transition
3. Vary tempo slightly for interest
4. Return to neutral settings between tracks
