## 🔧 Key Features

### Core Features
- ✅ Dual deck audio mixing
- ✅ 10-band equalizer
- ✅ Crossfader
- ✅ BPM and tempo control
- ✅ Master volume control
- ✅ Individual deck volume control
- ✅ Progress tracking and seeking

### Platform Support
- ✅ Windows 10+
- ✅ Android 5.0+

### Audio Support
- ✅ MP3 (320kbps recommended)
- ✅ WAV (lossless)
- ✅ FLAC (lossless)
- ✅ OGG (320kbps recommended)
- ✅ M4A (AAC)
- ✅ AAC
